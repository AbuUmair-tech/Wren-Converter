## Your files

Files you convert are opened, converted and saved by your own browser, on your own device. They are **not uploaded** to WrenConvert or to anyone else, and I have no way to see them. When you close or reload the page, the files and results are gone from the page's memory.

You can check this yourself: load a converter, switch off your internet connection, then convert a file. It still works, because nothing needs to be sent anywhere.

## What the site stores on your device

- **Your colour theme.** If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it stays the same next time.
- **A copy of the site's code.** The site's pages, styles, scripts and the converter libraries you use are kept in your browser's cache, so they load faster and work offline. This contains code only, never your files.

You can remove both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

WrenConvert does not use cookies, does not ask you to sign in, and does not fingerprint your browser.

## Analytics and advertising

The site currently uses **no analytics and no advertising**, and loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies. Everything comes from this website.

If that ever changes, for example to count visits or to show ads that pay for the site, this policy will be updated first, with the date at the top changed and the providers named here.

## Hosting

The site is served by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages). Like any web server, it may record technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, the page requested and the time, to keep the service running and secure. I don't receive these records for individual visitors. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you, I don't add it to any mailing list, and I don't share it with anyone.

## Children

The site does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because the site does not collect personal information about visitors, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have emailed me and want those messages deleted, ask and I will delete them.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be mentioned on the home page for a while.
