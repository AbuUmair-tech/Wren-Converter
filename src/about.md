## Why we built it

Most online converters ask you to upload a photo, a spreadsheet or a document to their server. That is slow, it puts limits on file size, and it means a copy of your file sits on a computer you know nothing about.

Modern browsers are powerful enough to do the converting themselves. WrenConvert uses that: every tool runs as code inside the page, on your phone or computer.

## Why a wren

The wren is one of the smallest birds around, and one of the loudest and busiest. Small, light and quick is what we want every tool on this site to be.
